Pioneering Research in AI & Distributed Computing

I'm a PhD student at Queensland University of Technology with extensive experience advancing the frontiers of computer networks and optimization algorithms. My research focuses on developing innovative solutions in distributed computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, with particular emphasis on mobile edge computing and reinforcement learning applications.

With publications in leading Q1 journals including Elsevier, IEEE, and Springer, I specialize in creating efficient task offloading strategies, resource allocation algorithms, and performance optimization techniques for emerging computing paradigms in IoT and vehicular networks.
