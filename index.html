About

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Computer Science at Queensland University of Technology, working with the Trusted Networks Lab on decentralised grid architectures for electricity distribution networks. My work sits between power systems and distributed computing: how coordination, privacy and trust should be arranged when generation, storage and demand are spread across thousands of edge participants.

Before QUT I spent several years on deep reinforcement learning for mobile and vehicular edge computing — task offloading, energy-aware resource allocation, and blockchain-backed security for connected vehicles. That thread continues in my current research on vehicular energy trading and EV charging coordination.
